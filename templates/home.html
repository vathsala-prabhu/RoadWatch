HOW ROADWATCH WORKS

End-to-end roadway intelligence

From citizen reporting to city maintenance resolution in 3 seamless steps.

📍

1. Accurate GPS Pinpointing

Use automatic browser geolocation or click directly on the interactive map with a draggable pin. Supports photos taken on-site or uploaded later from home.

🗺️

2. Interactive Community Map

Explore all verified road issues on OpenStreetMap. Filter by damage category and status with color-coded markers (Pending, In Progress, Resolved).

📊

3. Heatmaps & Analytics

Municipal teams visualize complaint clustering using density heatmaps, track monthly trends, and prioritize critical road repairs effectively.
